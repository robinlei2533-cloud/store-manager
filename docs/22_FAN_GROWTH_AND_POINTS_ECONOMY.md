# UWELL CRM Fan Growth And Points Economy

Date: 2026-07-17

## Purpose

This document defines the planned fan growth, points economy, membership journey, rewards, and activity freshness direction for UWELL CRM.

It is a product direction and future implementation reference. It does not mean every rule, dashboard, activity template, or fan-facing feature is already implemented.

## Growth Goal

Fan growth should increase trust in UWELL, create repeat engagement, guide fans to UWELL Brand Stores, and support terminal sell-through.

The fan system should not become only a points-mining tool. Points, levels, rewards, activities, scan, community, and S Store experiences should work together as a brand trust loop.

## Core Fan Growth Loop

```text
Fan joins UWELL
-> fan checks in, scans products, joins activities, posts, and redeems rewards
-> Available points create short-term reward motivation
-> Lifetime growth creates long-term membership status
-> rewards and activities guide fans to UWELL Brand Stores
-> stores verify pickup, activity, and service experience
-> backend sees points cost, reward cost, activity effect, scan signal, and S Store contribution
-> UWELL adjusts points, activities, rewards, store support, and product strategy
-> fan trust and terminal sell-through improve
```

## Points Model

UWELL should keep two point concepts:

```text
Available points = spendable points for reward redemption
Lifetime growth = accumulated growth score for membership level
```

Reward redemption deducts Available points.

Reward redemption does not deduct Lifetime growth.

Fan level is based on Lifetime growth.

Fan-facing explanation should stay simple:

```text
Spend points, keep your level.
```

## Points Source Categories

### 1. Light Engagement Points

Purpose:

- keep fans returning;
- support daily and community habits.

Examples:

- daily check-in;
- community like;
- community comment;
- official light tasks.

These points should stay low and capped.

### 2. Product Contact Points

Purpose:

- reward real UWELL product contact;
- support product authenticity trust.

Examples:

- valid UWELL product scan;
- product verification learning.

These points need scan risk protection.

### 3. Offline Store And Activity Points

Purpose:

- guide fans to stores;
- connect fan activity with terminal execution.

Examples:

- Store event check-in;
- Brand Store Event;
- store-verified campaign participation.

These points can be higher but need store verification.

### 4. Identity And Relationship Points

Purpose:

- reward high-value fan relationship signals.

Examples:

- valid invited fan registration;
- old fan verification.

These points should be limited and may require review or verification.

## Daily Routine Cap

Planned daily routine cap:

```text
Daily routine cap = 50 points/day
```

Included in the routine cap:

- check-in;
- scan counted points;
- community like/comment/post;
- official light tasks.

Not included in the routine cap:

- store activity rewards;
- invite rewards;
- old fan verification;
- backend-approved compensation;
- special campaign rewards.

## Category Caps

| Action | V1 cap |
|---|---|
| Check-in | 1/day |
| Scan counted points | 3/day |
| Community like | 10/day |
| Community comment | 5/day |
| Community post/share | 1/day |
| Store activity | 1 time per activity per fan |
| Invite | 1 time per valid invited fan |
| Old fan verification | 1 time per fan |

## Points Risk Direction

Risk Center should eventually flag suspicious points behavior.

Examples:

- fan frequently reaches the 50-point routine cap;
- points are concentrated in community or light tasks;
- short-time repeated point gains;
- point growth does not match normal scan, activity, or verification evidence.

High points from scans or store activities are not automatically bad. They should be evaluated with scan evidence, store verification, activity records, and S Store signals.

## Points Cost Metrics

Backend should eventually track:

- points issued;
- points redeemed;
- outstanding points;
- points by source;
- reward cost redeemed;
- cost per active fan;
- cost per store visit;
- cost per S Store pickup.

These metrics should come from real records, not invented dashboard numbers.

## Membership Journey

The current four-level structure should stay:

| Level | Lifetime growth |
|---|---:|
| Bronze | 0 |
| Silver | 300 |
| Gold | 1000 |
| Diamond | 5000 |

The level system should become a membership journey, not just a score label.

| Level | Fan identity |
|---|---|
| Bronze | New fan |
| Silver | Active fan |
| Gold | Core fan |
| Diamond | VIP fan |

## Membership Benefits Direction

| Level | Benefit direction |
|---|---|
| Bronze | Basic check-in, scan, normal activities, normal rewards, starter tasks |
| Silver | More normal rewards, some early activity visibility, ordinary Brand Store Events |
| Gold | Premium rewards, S Store activity priority, new product experience, member tasks |
| Diamond | High-value reward eligibility, VIP Brand Store Events, first-batch product experience, priority review/service, brand ambassador candidate |

Benefits should not all become physical gifts. Prefer low-cost, high-trust benefits such as:

- priority access;
- priority pickup;
- priority review;
- exclusive invitations;
- new product experience;
- membership identity display;
- S Store service priority.

## Growth Tasks

Fan growth pages should recommend concrete next actions.

Examples:

```text
To reach Gold:
- Scan UWELL products
- Join a Brand Store Event
- Redeem your first reward
- Visit a UWELL Brand Store
```

Growth tasks should guide real brand behaviors rather than only encourage passive point farming.

## Rewards System

Rewards should be separated into clear tiers.

| Reward tier | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Normal Rewards | Make points useful for new and regular fans |
| Premium Rewards | Encourage level growth and S Store visits |
| Diamond / High-value Rewards | Build high-trust and VIP brand feeling |
| Experience Rewards | Strengthen product and brand experience |

## Rewards And Membership

| Level | Reward access direction |
|---|---|
| Bronze | Normal |
| Silver | Normal + some Experience |
| Gold | Normal + Premium + Experience |
| Diamond | Normal + Premium + Diamond + VIP Experience |

## Rewards And S Stores

| Reward tier | S Store role |
|---|---|
| Normal | A/S stores can fulfill; S Stores are recommended first |
| Premium | S Stores are the main pickup point |
| Diamond / High-value | Backend review first, then S Store or backend-selected pickup |
| Experience | Usually happens at S Stores or Brand Store Events |

Do not add S Store exclusive rewards until company incentive policy is confirmed.

## Reward Cost And Risk

Backend should eventually track:

- reward cost;
- stock;
- redemption count;
- pickup count;
- unclaimed count;
- expired count;
- redemption by level;
- pickup by store;
- S Store pickup contribution.

High-value rewards must require review and should not auto-approve.

Reward risk should include:

- frequent high-value reward redemption;
- repeated high-value redemption by the same fan;
- abnormal pickup concentration at a store;
- reward attempts before review approval.

## Activity System

Activities should be brand experience and terminal conversion tools, not only point tasks.

Activity goals:

1. brand awareness;
2. product education;
3. S Store visits;
4. purchase-linked signals;
5. terminal feedback.

## Activity Types

| Activity type | Goal | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Light Engagement | Low-cost activity | Read article, visit website, follow social account, like/comment official post |
| Product Education | Brand and product trust | Authenticity learning, product feature intro, usage tips, open-system vs disposable learning |
| Brand Store Event | S Store visit | Store check-in, new product trial, offline experience, staff-guided product intro |
| Purchase-linked Activity | Sales-adjacent action | Buy and scan, store-verified purchase activity, buy-and-gift campaign |
| Feedback Activity | Terminal market feedback | Flavor vote, product feedback, consumer opinion, market preference |

## Activity Points Direction

| Activity type | Points direction |
|---|---:|
| Light Engagement | +5 to +10 |
| Product Education | +5 to +10 |
| Brand Store Event | +20 to +100 |
| Purchase-linked Activity | +10 to +100, subject to confirmed rules |
| Feedback Activity | +5 to +20 |

High-point activities should require verification or review.

## Campaign Freshness

Fan activity must stay fresh. The fan app should not rely only on static tasks.

The activity system should include:

### Always-on Activities

- daily check-in;
- product scan;
- starter tasks;
- product knowledge;
- basic community interaction.

### Weekly / Monthly Activities

- weekly flavor vote;
- weekly community challenge;
- monthly S Store check-in;
- monthly new product feedback;
- monthly Brand Store Event.

### Campaign / Launch Activities

- new product launch;
- holiday campaign;
- city campaign;
- S Store opening event;
- limited reward drop.

## Campaign Operations Direction

Backend should eventually support:

- campaign templates;
- campaign calendar;
- campaign states: Draft, Scheduled, Active, Ended, Archived;
- campaign recommendation slots;
- campaign performance metrics;
- campaign cost control;
- campaign review;
- campaign risk checks.

Suggested operating rhythm:

```text
Daily: always-on tasks
Weekly: at least one light activity
Monthly: at least one key activity
Launch/holiday: special campaign activity
```

## Activity Metrics

Backend should eventually track:

- activity views;
- joined users;
- completed users;
- store verifications;
- points issued;
- reward pickups after activity;
- scans after activity;
- S Store contribution;
- feedback collected.

Sell-through comparison can be added after S Store sell-through data becomes stable.

## Fan Growth And Governance

Fan Growth must connect to backend governance:

- high-value rewards go to Reviews;
- store-created activities go to Reviews;
- points anomalies go to Risk Center;
- scan anomalies go to Risk Center;
- reward anomalies go to Risk Center;
- critical rule and review changes write Audit Log.

## Recommended Implementation Order

1. Confirm points cap and membership rules in documentation.
2. Ensure existing fan rules do not conflict with the planned daily routine cap.
3. Add backend points-source and reward-cost visibility.
4. Improve membership benefit presentation.
5. Add reward tier clarity.
6. Add campaign freshness operations.
7. Connect points, reward, scan, and activity anomalies to Risk Center.

Each implementation task still requires impact analysis, affected files, database/API/permission review, and user confirmation before coding.
