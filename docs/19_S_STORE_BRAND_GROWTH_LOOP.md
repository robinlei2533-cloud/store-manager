# UWELL CRM S Store Brand Growth Loop

Date: 2026-07-17

## Purpose

This document defines the planned S Store brand-growth loop for UWELL CRM.

It is a product direction and future implementation reference. It does not mean the related pages, database tables, APIs, permissions, or dashboards already exist.

## Strategic Goal

UWELL CRM exists to help the brand directly reach terminal consumers and terminal stores.

Importers and wholesalers may remain important for capital flow, stock flow, and trade execution, but UWELL should not depend on them as the only source of terminal market truth.

The system should help UWELL understand:

- which stores are willing to represent the brand;
- which product types sell through at terminal stores;
- which activities bring fans to stores;
- which rewards create store visits;
- what consumers, store owners, and field reps are seeing in the market.

## Core Closed Loop

```text
UWELL selects and supports S Stores
-> S Stores present, recommend, and sell UWELL products
-> Fans discover S Stores, join activities, scan products, and pick up rewards
-> Stores submit sell-through, inventory, and material status
-> Field reps visit S Stores, replenish stock, and collect market feedback
-> Backend aggregates S Store health, sell-through, fan contribution, and market signals
-> UWELL adjusts products, replenishment, activities, rewards, training, and store support
-> S Stores sell more, fans trust more, and UWELL strengthens terminal brand control
```

## S Store Definition

An S Store is a **UWELL Brand Store**.

It is not simply a high-score store. It is a selected brand cooperation store that represents UWELL at the terminal.

An S Store may sell other brands, but it should actively recommend UWELL products and maintain UWELL brand display, culture, training, and activity execution.

UWELL may support S Stores with renovation, display upgrades, training, materials, activities, reward pickup flow, and future incentive policies.

## S Store Selection Standard

S Stores are selected from A-level stores.

Core selection criteria:

1. The store is already an A-level store.
2. The owner has strong cooperation willingness.
3. The owner is willing to recommend UWELL products.
4. The store is willing to cooperate with sell-through, inventory, field visits, reward pickup, and brand activities.
5. Objective conditions such as renovation, display, sales foundation, and training can be improved later with UWELL support.

## S Store Responsibilities

S Store responsibilities should stay light in the first version.

Store-side responsibilities:

1. Submit weekly and monthly sell-through for:
   - open-system products;
   - disposable products.
2. Submit store product inventory.
3. Submit store material inventory.
4. Maintain UWELL display, decoration, and brand culture.
5. Cooperate with reward pickup and brand-store activities.
6. Cooperate with staff training.

Data that can be generated by the system should not be manually duplicated by stores.

## Sell-through Rules

The first version should not require SKU-level sell-through.

Required store-submitted metrics:

| Period | Open-system products | Disposable products |
|---|---:|---:|
| Weekly | Sold quantity | Sold quantity |
| Monthly | Sold quantity | Sold quantity |

Backend should compare:

- this week vs last week open-system sales;
- this week vs last week disposable sales;
- this month vs last month open-system sales;
- this month vs last month disposable sales.

Sell-through data submitted by the store is locked after submission. Stores cannot edit historical submitted periods.

Corrections should be handled by backend users with an audit trail.

## Inventory Rules

S Stores submit:

- open-system current stock;
- disposable current stock;
- target stock for each product type;
- material inventory where applicable.

Low stock is triggered when:

```text
current stock <= target stock / 3
```

Low-stock records should trigger field-rep replenishment follow-up.

## Field Rep S Store Visit

Field reps are the main source of terminal market intelligence.

S Store visits should record:

- inventory status;
- display status;
- sell-through observation;
- competitor situation;
- hot market brands;
- hot flavors;
- consumer feedback;
- latest market situation;
- support needed;
- replenishment need;
- visit photos;
- replenishment completion photos when applicable.

Field rep visit records do not need review every time, but backend users can correct data when needed. Corrections should leave an audit trail.

## Replenishment Follow-up

Replenishment is handled by field reps.

The flow should be:

```text
Store submits inventory
-> system detects low stock
-> backend marks low-stock S Store
-> field rep follows up
-> field rep replenishes stock and uploads photos
-> replenishment status becomes Completed
```

## S Store Status

Suggested statuses:

- Active
- Needs follow-up
- Paused
- Downgraded
- Under review

Manager and Admin can manage S Store status.

## Downgrade Rules

An S Store can be directly downgraded to A when any of the following occurs:

1. UWELL products are out of stock for more than one month.
2. The store is unreachable for more than one month.
3. The owner cooperation attitude clearly declines.
4. The store is no longer willing to act as a UWELL Brand Store.

Downgrade records must keep:

- downgrade time;
- operator;
- reason;
- before status;
- after status;
- notes;
- whether future re-evaluation is allowed.

## Restore Rules

A downgraded S Store returns to A-level status.

It can later reapply or be re-evaluated by Manager/Admin.

Restore rules:

1. The store must meet the same S Store selection standard.
2. At least one natural month of observation is recommended before restoration.
3. Restoration records should keep time, operator, reason, observation notes, and related downgrade record.

## Backend S Store Management

The first version should live under:

```text
Backend > Stores > S Store Management
```

It should include:

1. S Store Overview
2. S Store List
3. S Store Detail

S Store Detail should aggregate:

- basic store info;
- S Store status;
- sell-through data;
- store product inventory;
- material inventory;
- field visit notes;
- display and renovation photos;
- activity and fan contribution;
- replenishment follow-up;
- downgrade and restore history;
- future incentive policy slot.

## Store App S Store Report

Only S Stores should see S Store reporting entry points.

The store app should allow S Stores to submit:

- weekly open-system sold quantity;
- weekly disposable sold quantity;
- monthly open-system sold quantity;
- monthly disposable sold quantity;
- product inventory;
- material inventory.

Historical submitted records are read-only for stores.

## Fan App UWELL Brand Store Display

Fan-facing S Stores should be presented as:

```text
UWELL Brand Store
```

Fan-facing wording should not require fans to understand internal S/A/B/C rating logic.

Suggested fan-facing store labels:

| Backend level | Fan-facing label |
|---|---|
| S | UWELL Brand Store |
| A | Recommended Store |
| B/C | Store |

S Stores should be prioritized in fan store discovery when relevant and may show:

- official display;
- trained staff;
- reward pickup;
- new product experience;
- store photos;
- active UWELL activities.

## Reward Pickup And S Stores

Reward pickup should support S Store priority.

V1 direction:

1. Normal rewards can be fulfilled by A/S stores, with S Stores recommended first.
2. Premium rewards should prioritize S Stores.
3. Diamond or high-value rewards require backend review and may be directed to S Stores or backend-selected pickup stores.
4. Reward pickup records should count toward S Store contribution when fulfilled at an S Store.

Do not add S Store exclusive rewards until company incentive policy is confirmed.

## Brand Store Events

Official brand activities should prioritize S Stores when the activity needs terminal execution.

Suitable S Store activities:

- new product trial;
- store check-in;
- offline experience;
- holiday event;
- buy-and-gift event;
- consumer feedback collection;
- hot flavor voting.

Fan-facing activity label:

```text
Brand Store Event
```

Activity contribution should count:

- activity count;
- participation count;
- store verification count;
- points issued;
- post-activity scans;
- reward pickups.

## Scan Signal And S Store Contribution

Product scan remains a lightweight fan action and should not force store binding.

V1 direction:

1. Normal product scans do not require store selection.
2. S Store activity codes and check-in codes automatically bind to the S Store.
3. After successful normal scan, fans may optionally select where they got the product.
4. Store-linked scans count toward S Store contribution.
5. Unlinked scans count as product or regional heat only.
6. Suspicious scans do not count toward S Store contribution.
7. Scan signals support sell-through analysis but do not replace S Store sell-through reporting.

## Brand Growth / S Store Overview

The first version should be placed under:

```text
Backend > Stores > S Store Management > Overview
```

V1 metrics:

1. Active S Stores
2. New S Stores this month
3. Downgraded S Stores this month
4. Low-stock S Stores
5. Weekly open-system sales
6. Weekly disposable sales
7. Monthly open-system sales
8. Monthly disposable sales
9. Pending replenishment
10. Completed replenishment
11. Brand Store event verifications
12. Reward pickups at S Stores

Later versions can add:

- S Store contribution ranking;
- regional performance;
- before/after activity sell-through comparison;
- reward pickup to sell-through comparison;
- hot brand and hot flavor summaries;
- consumer feedback keywords;
- field rep visit completion rate;
- S Store ROI.

## Permissions Direction

Planned permission direction:

| Role | S Store permission |
|---|---|
| Store Owner | Submit new sell-through, product inventory, and material inventory for own S Store; cannot edit history |
| Field Rep | Submit S Store visit records, replenishment status, and photos for assigned stores |
| Manager | Manage S Store status in assigned region, correct data with audit trail |
| Admin | Full S Store management and audit access |
| Fan | View fan-facing UWELL Brand Store information only |

## Implementation Roadmap

Recommended order:

1. Document S Store product rules.
2. Confirm data fields and permissions.
3. Build Backend > Stores > S Store Management.
4. Add Store App > S Store Report.
5. Upgrade Field Visits with S Store visit template.
6. Add replenishment follow-up.
7. Add S Store downgrade and restore records.
8. Add Brand Growth / S Store Overview.

Each implementation task must still follow the project workflow: analyze impact, list affected files, state database/API/permission impact, and wait for user confirmation before development.
