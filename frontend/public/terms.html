UWELL Member Terms and Reward Rules

Last updated: July 7, 2026

These terms apply to the UWELL Fans Club, Store Portal, CRM operations portal, points program, product scans, and S-level store reward pickup trial.

1. Eligibility

You must be of legal age in your region to use the service. By registering or signing in, you confirm that your account information is accurate and that you are legally allowed to participate.

2. Account Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for keeping your email, password, phone number, and account access secure.
  • You must not impersonate another person, use another fan account, or create accounts to abuse points or rewards.
  • UWELL may suspend accounts that show fraud, duplicated activity, fake data, or other misuse.

3. Points

  • Points may be earned through approved actions such as check-in, verified product scans, campaign participation, and UWELL-approved operations.
  • Points have no cash value and cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged for cash, or traded outside the platform.
  • UWELL may adjust, reverse, freeze, or cancel points created by error, fraud, duplicated scans, screenshots, automation, or system abuse.

4. Reward Redemption Rules

  • When a fan redeems a reward, points are deducted and a unique redemption code is generated.
  • The redemption code must be shown in the Fan Center and verified in the Store Portal before pickup.
  • Only approved S-level UWELL stores may fulfill fan rewards.
  • Each redemption code can be used once. After successful pickup, the code cannot be reused.
  • The default validity period is 7 days from redemption unless a campaign states a shorter period.
  • Rewards are subject to stock availability. If a store has no stock, the fan should visit another S-level store or contact support.
  • UWELL may cancel suspicious redemptions, copied codes, expired codes, altered screenshots, or off-platform reward trades.

5. S-Level Store Responsibility

  • S-level stores must verify the code in the Store Portal before handing over a reward.
  • The store must confirm the displayed reward name and quantity match the physical item being handed to the fan.
  • Pickup confirmation should happen only after the fan receives the reward in store.
  • Successful confirmation deducts the corresponding reward stock. Stores must keep reward inventory accurate.
  • Stores must not fulfill expired, already used, copied, manually altered, or off-platform redemption codes.
  • Suspicious pickup attempts must be escalated to UWELL operations instead of being completed privately.

6. S-Level Store Rewards and Consequences

  • S-level stores receive priority access to official reward inventory, display materials, and campaign support.
  • Monthly pickup records may be used for store ranking, campaign invitations, and service incentives.
  • Stores with clean pickup records and accurate inventory may receive priority UWELL lightbox, acrylic stand, or campaign benefits.
  • Incorrect confirmations, private redemption, inventory mismatch, or extra charges for official rewards may reduce store rating or suspend reward fulfillment permission.

7. Store Registration and Review

Store registration, store level, and reward fulfillment permission are subject to UWELL review. UWELL may approve, reject, downgrade, suspend, or remove store permissions based on operational quality, inventory accuracy, display quality, or rule violations.

8. Service Changes

UWELL may update points rules, reward availability, store eligibility, campaign rules, and trial features with reasonable notice. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated terms.

9. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact legal@uwell.com.
