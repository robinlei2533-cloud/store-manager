UWELL Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This notice explains how UWELL processes personal data for the UWELL Fans Club, Store Portal, CRM operations portal, reward redemption, product scans, and related trial services.

1. Data Controller and Contact

UWELL is responsible for operating the platform and deciding why and how personal data is processed. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@uwell.com.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, country, city, password credentials, account role, store name, and store contact details.
  • Membership data: fan ID, points balance, fan level, check-in records, scan records, campaign participation, invitations, community activity, and support requests.
  • Reward data: redeemed item, redemption code, pickup status, pickup store, stock movement, pickup time, and anti-fraud review notes.
  • Store operation data: store level, inventory, material requests, display uploads, visit records, campaign claims, and reward fulfillment records.
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, IP address, approximate location derived from user input, security logs, and diagnostic events.

3. Why We Use Personal Data

  • To create and secure fan, store, and operations accounts.
  • To run points, product scans, campaigns, store recommendations, and reward pickup.
  • To verify redemption codes at S-level stores and prevent duplicate or fraudulent pickup.
  • To manage store inventory, material support, display quality, and store level responsibilities.
  • To provide customer support, service notices, and important account messages.
  • To improve the platform using aggregated performance and usage analytics.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, audit, and security obligations.

4. Legal Basis

We process personal data where it is necessary to provide the requested service, perform account and reward operations, comply with legal obligations, protect platform security, or based on your consent where consent is required by applicable law.

5. Sharing and Recipients

We do not sell personal data. We may share relevant data with authorized UWELL distributors, S-level store partners, cloud and authentication providers, analytics and support vendors, auditors, regulators, or law enforcement where legally required. Store partners see only the information needed to verify and fulfill rewards.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep account, points, redemption, and store operation records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, support audits, and meet legal requirements. When data is no longer needed, we delete, anonymize, or securely archive it according to operational and legal retention needs.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards such as access controls, authentication, role permissions, logging, and operational review. No online service is risk-free, so users and store partners must keep account credentials confidential.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion where legally available, restriction or objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and information about how your data is used. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. International Processing

UWELL services may use cloud infrastructure and authorized service providers in different countries. Where data is transferred internationally, we apply appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this notice as the trial service, legal requirements, or operating model changes. Material changes will be shown in the service or communicated through appropriate channels.
