UWELL Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

UWELL ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the UWELL Fans Club, Store Portal, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, phone number, country, and city when you register.
  • Usage Data: Scan records, point transactions, campaign participation, and store visit records.
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, and IP address for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services (membership, points, rewards)
  • Verify product authenticity through QR code scanning
  • Send important updates about your account and promotions
  • Improve our products and services based on usage analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Authorized UWELL distributors and store partners for reward fulfillment
  • Service providers who assist in operating our platform
  • Law enforcement when required by applicable law

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@uwell.com.
